# Natural Disaster Information for Innovation Academy

This document outlines the structure and content for the natural disaster information pages for the Innovation Academy school website.

### General Requirements

*   **Technology Stack**: HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
*   **Styling**: Adhere to the Innovation Academy's color theme (provided separately).
*   **Responsiveness**: Pages should be responsive and display well on various devices (desktop, tablet, mobile).
*   **Content Pages**: All content pages (`before-during-after.html`, `status.html`, `supplies.html`, `types.html`) will focus solely on their respective content, without including the main header and navigation elements. These pages are designed to be integrated into a larger website structure.
*   **Accessibility**: Ensure basic accessibility standards are met (e.g., alt text for images, semantic HTML).
*   **Performance**: Optimize images and code for fast loading times.

### Page Breakdown and Content Requirements

#### 1. Before, During, and After Natural Disasters (before-during-after.html)

*   **Purpose**: Provide comprehensive guidance on actions to take before, during, and after various natural disasters.
*   **Content Sections**:
    *   **Introduction**: Emphasize the importance of preparedness.
    *   **Before a Disaster**: Detailed checklist for preparation (e.g., emergency kit, family plan, home safety).
    *   **During a Disaster**: Specific actions for different disaster types (e.g., tornado, flood, severe storm).
    *   **After a Disaster**: Steps for recovery and safety post-disaster (e.g., checking for injuries, contacting family, returning home).
    *   **Visuals**: Include relevant images or icons for each section (e.g., emergency kit, shelter, recovery).

#### 2. Status Page (status.html)

*   **Purpose**: Display real-time or near real-time information regarding natural disaster alerts, school status, and local conditions.
*   **Content Sections**:
    *   **Current Alerts & Warnings**: Placeholder for severe weather alerts (e.g., tornado watch/warning, flood advisory).
    *   **School Status: FCS Innovation Academy**: Placeholder for school closures, delays, or operational changes.
    *   **Local Conditions & Resources**: Placeholder for local weather forecasts, road closures, and emergency contact information.
    *   **Visuals**: Use clear indicators (e.g., color-coded status, icons) for quick understanding. Include images for alerts, school status, and local conditions.

#### 3. Basic Supplies (supplies.html)

*   **Purpose**: List essential supplies for an emergency kit with explanations for each item.
*   **Content Sections**:
    *   **Introduction**: Explain the importance of an emergency kit.
    *   **Water**: Quantity, storage, and purification methods.
    *   **Food**: Non-perishable items, dietary considerations, and preparation.
    *   **First-Aid Kit**: Essential medical supplies and medications.
    *   **Tools & Utilities**: Flashlights, radios, wrenches, etc.
    *   **Important Documents & Cash**: Copies of vital papers and emergency funds.
    *   **Special Items**: Considerations for infants, pets, and comfort items.
    *   **Visuals**: Include images for each category of supplies (e.g., water, food, first-aid kit).

#### 4. Types of Natural Disasters (types.html)

*   **Purpose**: Describe various natural disasters relevant to the Alpharetta area, their characteristics, and safety measures.
*   **Content Sections**:
    *   **Severe Thunderstorms & Tornadoes**: Description, threats, preparation, and during-event actions.
    *   **Flooding**: Description, causes, threats, preparation, and during-event actions.
    *   **Winter Storms & Ice Events**: Description, threats, preparation, and during-event actions.
    *   **Drought & Wildfires**: Description, threats, preparation, and during-event actions.
    *   **Heatwaves**: Description, threats, preparation, and during-event actions.
    *   **Visuals**: Include relevant images for each type of disaster.
