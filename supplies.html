Basic Supplies for Emergency Preparedness

Being prepared for an emergency means having the essential supplies to sustain yourself and your family for at least 72 hours. This list outlines basic items recommended for every emergency kit.

Water and Food

  • Water: One gallon per person per day for at least three days, for drinking and sanitation.
  • Food: At least a three-day supply of non-perishable food.

First Aid and Medication

  • First Aid Kit: Include bandages, antiseptic wipes, gauze, adhesive tape, pain relievers, etc.
  • Prescription Medications: At least a 7-day supply, along with any necessary medical supplies.

Tools and Utilities

  • Flashlight: With extra batteries.
  • Whistle: To signal for help.
  • Multi-purpose Tool: Can include pliers, screwdriver, can opener.
  • Manual Can Opener: For food.
  • Cell Phone: With chargers and a backup battery/power bank.

Clothing and Bedding

  • Blankets or Sleeping Bags: One per person.
  • Complete Change of Clothing: Including long-sleeved shirts, long pants, and sturdy shoes.

Important Documents and Cash

  • Copies of Important Documents: Identification, insurance policies, bank records in a waterproof portable container.
  • Cash: Small bills, as ATMs and credit card machines may not work during power outages.

Special Items

  • Infant Formula and Diapers: If you have infants.
  • Pet Food and Extra Water: For your pet.

Sanitation and Hygiene

  • Feminine Hygiene Supplies: And personal sanitation items.
  • Moist Towelettes, Garbage Bags and Plastic Ties: For personal sanitation.
  • Dust Mask: To help filter contaminated air.

Additional Considerations

  • Emergency Blanket: For warmth.
  • Plastic Sheeting and Duct Tape: To shelter-in-place.
  • Wrench or Pliers: To turn off utilities.
  • Local Maps: In case GPS is unavailable.
